About this receiver

This dial is built from a research registry of political broadcasts gathered for a film: town councils, regional assemblies, national parliaments, political shows, news channels and think tanks. Politics never stops talking. Almost nobody is listening to most of it.

    How to read it

    • Rooms runs from town councils, through regions and nations, up to world bodies.
    • Voices runs from left to right. The labels come from the research registry and have not been independently reviewed. Most of them are American.
    • Wire holds news channels, think tanks and shows without a left or right label.
    • A lit tick has a broadcast. A dim tick is a dark station: the room exists, but we found no channel we could verify.

    What you are watching

    Each station plays from that body's own YouTube channel, through YouTube's player. Most of it is recent recordings, not live. Nothing is re-hosted, and the receiver drops you into the middle of a broadcast on purpose.

    Prototype. Nearly half of the registry is from the United States, because that is where the research was done from.
